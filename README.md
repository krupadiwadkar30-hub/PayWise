# PayWise – Spend Smart, Stay Wise
**PayWise** is a smart personal financial management application designed to help users manage their salary and EMIs, track everyday expenses, control spending, understand monthly financial patterns, and make smarter financial decisions.

## Features
* User Sign Up and Login
* Personal financial information
* Monthly salary management
* Add and manage EMIs
* View EMI details and due dates
* EMI-to-income ratio
* Remaining salary calculation
* Financial status based on EMI commitments
* Everyday expense tracking
* Category-wise expense tracking
* Monthly spending limits
* Smart spending alerts
* EMI payment reminders and notifications
* **What Should I Cut?** saving suggestions
* **What If?** financial simulation
* **Monthly Insights**
* **Smart Financial Decisions**
* Profile management
* Customisable Settings
* Country and currency selection
* Multi-language support
* Support for **14 languages**

## Problem Statement
Managing a monthly salary along with multiple EMIs and everyday expenses can make it difficult to understand where money is actually being spent.
Users may know their income and EMI commitments, but they can still struggle to understand their everyday spending patterns and how those expenses affect their overall financial situation.

## Our Solution
PayWise brings important financial activities together in one place and presents them in a simple and organised way.
PayWise helps users:
* Manage their monthly salary
* Track their EMIs
* Monitor upcoming EMI due dates
* Understand their EMI-to-income ratio
* Calculate their remaining salary
* Track everyday spending
* Analyse category-wise expenses
* Set monthly spending limits
* Receive smart spending alerts
* Identify areas where they can potentially reduce expenses
* Explore possible financial changes using **What If?**
* Review their spending history using **Monthly Insights**
* Understand their spending patterns
* Get actionable guidance through **Smart Financial Decisions**
* Customise their financial management experience

## Key Features
### Everyday Expenses
Users can record expenses under categories such as:
* Food
* Travel
* Shopping
* Bills
* Entertainment
* Other
PayWise automatically calculates total spending and provides a category-wise breakdown.
Users can also set spending limits for individual categories and receive smart alerts when spending approaches or crosses a limit.

### Monthly Insights
Monthly Insights allows users to review their spending history across different months.
Users can view:
* Total monthly spending
* Category-wise spending
* Spending patterns over time
This helps users identify changes in their spending behaviour and understand where their money is going from month to month.

### Smart Financial Decisions
Smart Financial Decisions uses information such as salary, EMI commitments, expenses, spending limits, and spending patterns to provide understandable and actionable financial guidance.
The goal is to help users move from simply **tracking their money** to **understanding and managing it better**.

### Smart Notifications and Reminders
PayWise provides reminders for upcoming EMI payments and allows users to customise notification and reminder preferences.
This helps users stay aware of important financial commitments without having to repeatedly check the application.
